I am a first-year CS PhD student at <a href="https://ai.stanford.edu/">Stanford University</a>, advised by <a href="https://cs.stanford.edu/people/jure/">Prof. Jure Leskovec</a>. I am interested in the computational aspects of learning in neural networks. My research is supported by the Stanford School of Engineering Fellowship.

Previously I was a senior ML engineer at LinkedIn AI, building foundation models for recommendation. I earned my MSc in Computer Science at NYU Courant, where I was advised by <a href="https://cims.nyu.edu/~bruna/">Prof. Joan Bruna</a> in the Math and Data Group. I have also contributed to TensorFlow and maintained TensorFlow-IO at IBM, and hold a B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering from IIT Guwahati.
